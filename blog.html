    Why Automated Tests Pay Off

    A solid regression suite catches breakage before your users do.

    Structuring a MERN App

    Keep your API, data layer and UI concerns cleanly separated.

    Adding an LLM to Your Workflow

    LLMs are great at reviewing diffs and drafting test plans.

    Feeding a pull request's diff to a model and asking for edge cases is a cheap way to catch bugs a human reviewer might skim past.
